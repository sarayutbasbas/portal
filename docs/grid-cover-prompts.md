## Shared prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create one brand new standalone portrait cover artwork for the app in Basnana Portal. Portrait 2:3, full bleed, polished detailed illustration. Designed for a narrow mobile card about 150 × 230 px. Strong focal forms, main subject in the middle 20–65% of the image height. Quiet upper-right corner for the circular link icon. Dark, quiet bottom 30% for overlaid white app title and description. Keep key objects inside the central 70% of width for desktop crops. No text, lettering, title, logo, watermark, UI, borders, panels or collage. Artwork only, not a screenshot.
